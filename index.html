Why Free Tiers Surprise You

Silent Expiration

Azure's 12-month free services don't stop when they expire—they just start charging you. AWS monthly limits just reset and keep going.

Hidden Dependencies

A "free" VM triggers billable disks, IPs, and bandwidth. AWS Lambda needs S3, CloudWatch, and more. One service becomes five charges.

No Hard Stops

Both clouds let you exceed limits and bill you later. No warnings, no budget locks by default. You find out when the invoice arrives.

Everything You Need

🎯 Forgotten resources

azguard resources lists idle disks, unassociated public IPs, NAT gateways, stopped-but-billed VMs, and the AWS equivalents, with a monthly list-price estimate. The command is read-only.

📋 Free tier and spend

azguard status and azguard scan check Azure. azguard aws checks AWS. The limits files cover 12 Azure services and 13 AWS services. azguard cost shows the current month by service.

⚠️ Alerts

azguard budget stores an Azure threshold from $1 to $100, and azguard status prints it when you run the command. azguard watch polls for new billable resources every 15 minutes and can notify your desktop or a webhook. azguard guard checks billable commands against a session budget.

🧹 Cleanup Coming soon

Automatic cleanup is coming soon. Today, azguard cleanup prints a static guide and leaves your cloud resources in place.
